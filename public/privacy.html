Privacy

How we handle what you send us.

What we collect

When you submit the quote form we receive your name, ZIP code, email address, phone number, and whatever details you type in. Our web host, Cloudflare, also processes standard request data such as your IP address in order to serve the site and block automated abuse.

Why we collect it

To answer your request, to obtain quotes from insurance carriers on your behalf, and to service any policy you decide to buy through us.

Who we share it with

Insurance carriers and their underwriting systems, as needed to quote or place your coverage, and service providers who help us operate: web hosting, email, and agency management software. We do not sell your personal information.

Calls and text messages

If you tick the consent box on the quote form, you agree that we may contact you by phone, text, or email about your request. You can opt out of texts by replying STOP, or ask us to stop calling at any time.

Sensitive information

Please do not send Social Security numbers, driver's licence numbers, or dates of birth through the website form. Where a carrier requires that information we will collect it by phone or through a secure link.

Your choices

Write to privacy@longhornshield.org to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to request deletion. Texas residents have additional rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act, and we will honour those requests within the time the law allows.
