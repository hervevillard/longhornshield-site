Auto insurance

Daily drivers, work trucks, brand-new teen drivers, motorcycles, boats and RVs — quoted across several carriers so you can see the real spread.

  • LiabilityPays for injury and property damage you cause. Texas minimums are low enough that one serious accident can blow straight past them; we will show you what higher limits actually cost, which is usually less than people fear.
  • Collision and comprehensiveDamage to your own vehicle, whether from a wreck, a hailstorm, a thief, or a deer with poor judgement. Hail alone makes comprehensive worth pricing almost everywhere in this state.
  • Uninsured and underinsured motoristCovers you when the other driver has nothing, or nowhere near enough. Declined by accident more often than any other coverage, and regretted more often too.
  • Personal injury protectionMedical bills and lost wages for you and your passengers, no matter who caused it. Carriers have to offer it in Texas and you have to decline it in writing.
  • Roadside and rental reimbursementTowing, lockouts, and something to drive while yours is in the shop.
  • Gap and new car replacementFor financed or leased vehicles where the loan is bigger than the car is worth. Worth a conversation the moment you sign for a new one.

What actually moves your auto premium here

Where it sleeps at night

Rates move by ZIP code, not by city. Two addresses fifteen minutes apart can price very differently on theft, claim, and hail history alone.

Mileage and the commute

Moved house, changed jobs, started working from home? Your policy may still be rated on a commute you stopped making three years ago.

Credit-based insurance score

Texas allows it and it carries real weight. If your credit has improved since you last shopped, that by itself is a reason to re-quote.

Old claims aging off

Most surcharges drop away after three to five years. Very few carriers will re-rate you unprompted when they do.
