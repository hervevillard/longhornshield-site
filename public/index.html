Long before anyone wrote a policy, a herd was how a family survived a bad year.

The horns in our mark belong to the Ankole, the East African longhorn that families have kept for centuries as their store of value against drought, illness, and hard luck. It sits inside a laced Nguni shield, and the woven trim running across this page keeps the company of the strip-weavers of West Africa.

Two continents, the same idea: put something sturdy between your family and a bad day. We do it with paperwork now, and we would rather explain the paperwork than hide behind it.

Why an independent agent, and not one company's answer

We compare, you decide

A captive agent has exactly one set of rates to offer you. We quote several and lay the trade-offs side by side, including what each carrier tends to do at renewal.

Bundling, only when it is really cheaper

Putting auto and home together usually helps. Not always, and not at every carrier. We check instead of assuming.

The fine print gets read out loud

Roof payment schedules, wind and hail percentages, and named-storm language decide what you actually get paid. We go through yours before you buy, not after a storm.

You keep the same person

Whoever writes your policy is who picks up when you have a claim, add a vehicle, or need a certificate by this afternoon.

How it goes

About ten minutes of back and forth for most people. Nothing is binding until you say the word.

  1. Send the basics

    Vehicles, an address, or what the business does — and your current policy if it is within reach.

  2. We shop it

    Your details go to our carriers and come back as real numbers, not a range with a question mark.

  3. We walk the options

    Phone or email, your call, including what each deductible would actually cost you in a claim.

  4. We handle the switch

    New policy set up, old one cancelled on the right date, proof of cover sent wherever it needs to land.
